# DESIGN SYSTEM — Digital Marketing & SEO Specialist Portfolio

## 1. Design principles
- Professional credibility over decoration.
- Clear communication and simple navigation.
- Strong hierarchy and concise content.
- Evidence-led portfolio presentation.
- Accessible, responsive, and bilingual-friendly layouts.

## 2. Visual direction
Create a premium personal-brand portfolio with a clean light background, deep navy typography, and a restrained blue/teal accent. The visual language should feel modern, strategic, and approachable. Avoid excessive gradients, neon colors, glassmorphism, oversized animation, and generic corporate stock photography.

## 4. Typography
- Use a modern sans-serif with full Bangla glyph support.
- Suggested pairing: Inter for Latin and Hind Siliguri for Bangla, with system fallbacks.
- Body: 16–18px, line-height around 1.5–1.7.
- Headings: bold but not excessively heavy; responsive scale.
- Keep paragraph line length comfortable.
- Ensure Bangla headings do not clip or wrap awkwardly.

## 5. Layout and spacing
- Centered content container: approximately 1120–1200px max width.
- Desktop gutters: 32–48px; tablet: 24px; mobile: 16–20px.
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 80px.
- Use generous section spacing and consistent alignment.
- Cards: 12–18px radius, subtle border, restrained shadow.

## 6. Responsive behavior
### Mobile
- Single-column hero and content sections.
- Collapsible navigation.
- Full-width CTA where appropriate.
- Skills and service cards stack cleanly.
- Portfolio cards use readable image crops and text.

### Tablet
- Two-column layouts where space permits.
- Keep touch targets comfortable.
- Avoid cramped multi-column text.

### Desktop
- Hero may use two columns: copy and portrait/abstract visual.
- Services and portfolio can use 2–3 column grids.
- Maintain consistent content width and whitespace.

## 7. Components
### Header
- Editable wordmark / owner name.
- Home, About, Skills, Services, Portfolio, Contact.
- Language switcher.
- Primary CTA.

### Hero
- Professional title and value proposition.
- One primary and one secondary CTA.
- Owner portrait only if supplied; otherwise use an abstract visual.

### Skill group
- Group skills by category.
- Use text labels or restrained icons.
- Avoid unsupported percentage bars or proficiency scores.

### Service card
- Service name, short description, optional scope bullets, contact CTA.
- No invented pricing.

### Portfolio card
- Project image, project title, category/industry if permitted, short summary, “View Project”.
- No fabricated client identity or results.

### Case study
- Clear sections for context, challenge, approach, deliverables, outcome, and lessons.
- Mark unavailable information as omitted, not fabricated.

### Contact form
- Persistent labels, required/optional indicators, validation, and submission feedback.
- Accessible error messages and keyboard support.

### Footer
- Confirmed email, WhatsApp, and social profile links only.
- Navigation and copyright line with correct year generated by the application.

## 8. Imagery
- Prefer genuine owner portrait and genuine project screenshots.
- Use abstract marketing/SEO illustrations only as supporting visuals.
- Never show fabricated analytics, ranking screenshots, client dashboards, or certificates as proof.
- Use consistent image ratios and optimized WebP/AVIF assets.

## 9. Motion
- Subtle transitions only.
- Avoid scroll-triggered motion that delays content.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep focus and active states visible without animation dependence.

## 10. Accessibility
- Semantic landmarks and correct heading order.
- Keyboard navigation and visible focus ring.
- Contrast-compliant text and controls.
- Useful alt text for meaningful images.
- Decorative images should have empty alt.
- Do not place essential text inside images.

## 11. Brand setup checklist
- Confirm owner name and final professional title.
- Confirm brand/portfolio name and logo.
- Confirm palette and portrait.
- Confirm skills, services, and project content.
- Confirm contact and social URLs.
